title: "Explaining flood economic losses with machine learning"
tag: New paper
icon: flood
---
Rainfall and average temperature carry the most weight when a machine learning model estimates the economic loss of floods in Spain, at every loss level. For severe and catastrophic floods, socioeconomic variables related to the BRIC framework also stand out, namely housing and infrastructure, community capital and economic resilience.

The model uses insured direct economic losses, weather records and 32 resilience indicators from official statistics, and classifies 2,867 floods as moderate, severe or catastrophic.

Tecnun Resilience Group, Universidad de Navarra Published in the International Journal of Disaster Risk Reduction
